What we deliver

Four disciplines.
Proven in the field.

📋

Project Management

We lead and support complex technical programmes from initiation through delivery. Our project managers bring structure, risk awareness, and stakeholder discipline to environments where schedule and cost overruns have real consequences. We work within established frameworks — including PRINCE2, PMP, and agile-hybrid approaches — adapting to the client's context.

🏭

Production Management

For programmes with a production or manufacturing dimension, MAGTECH provides experienced consultants who understand the interplay between engineering design, supply chain, quality assurance, and schedule. We help organisations build and sustain production capability for high-value, low-volume systems.

📐

Requirements Management

Poor requirements are the root cause of most project failures. We establish and manage requirements processes that create clear traceability from customer need to delivered capability — reducing rework, managing change effectively, and providing the foundation for rigorous verification and validation.

✅

Test-Driven Development

In safety-critical and regulated environments, verification cannot be an afterthought. Our consultants embed test thinking early in development — defining acceptance criteria before implementation, building traceable test cases from requirements, and ensuring that what is delivered can be proven to meet its specification.

Founder & Lead Consultant

Henrik Magnusson —
the person behind MAGTECH

HM
Henrik Magnusson Founder & Lead Consultant · Uppsala, Sweden M.Sc. Computer Science · Technical Officer & Defence Engineer

Henrik brings over 20 years of senior leadership experience across space, defence, and telecommunications — not as an outside adviser, but as the person who has held the accountability, managed the teams, and delivered the results. He has led high-technology production sites, managed complex R&D programmes, run department P&Ls, and driven global transformation initiatives across multiple industries and continents.

His career spans Production & Site Manager and Senior Project Manager at Unibap Space Solutions, Interim Head of Propulsion at GomSpace — where he was the primary ESA interface across several international programmes — Global Change Driver at Ericsson, and twelve years at FMV (Swedish Defence Materiel Administration) as Project Manager and System Architect on mission-critical communication systems for the Swedish Armed Forces.

Henrik holds an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Communication Systems & Networking) and a Technical Officer and Defence Engineer qualification in Command & Control and Communication Systems. He is fluent in Swedish and English.
